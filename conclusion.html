Conclusion

Here are my conclusions on complexity/simplicity in engineering design:

Form of Representation -- Simple Yet Nuanced

In my portfolio, I have tried to balance simple representation with complex meanings. I have tried to be “simple” and concise with my portfolio presentation by using simple layouts and non-technical language, so as to not obscure meaning. I am also trying to capture the holistic praxis experience by including all the reflections that had a major impact on me.

Summary and Next Steps

Praxis is a holistic course with a lot of content, but my main takeaways from the course was that complexity has value, but so does simplicity. My design experiences have shown me there is always a balance to be made between embracing complexity and making simplifications -- going back to the Hoover Dam Model, as we model the world, we unavoidably have to make simplifications to build something useful. Knowing what simplifications to make and not make, however, requires a lot more learning and experience. Therefore, although I know that I still struggle with communicating concisely, Praxis has given me the tools to self-improve in further design projects. I will try to, as they say, move through complexity to get to simplicity.
