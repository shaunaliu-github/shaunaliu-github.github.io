Conclusion

Here are my conclusions on complexity and simplicity in engineering design:

Form of Representation for Portfolio -- Simple Yet Nuanced

In my portfolio, I have tried to balance simple representation with complex meanings. I have tried to be “simple” and concise with my portfolio presentation by using simple layouts and non-technical language, so as to not obscure meaning. I am also trying to capture the holistic Praxis experience by including all the reflections that had a major impact on me.

Summary and Next Steps

Praxis is a holistic course with a lot of content, but my main takeaway from the course was that complexity has value, but so does simplicity. My design experiences have shown me that there is always a balance to be made between embracing complexity and making simplifications -- going back to the Hoover Dam Model, as we model the world, we unavoidably have to make simplifications to build something useful. Knowing which simplifications to make and which to avoid, however, requires a lot more learning and experience. Therefore, although I know that I still struggle with communicating concisely, Praxis has given me the tools to improve myself in further design projects. I will try to, as they say, move through complexity to get to simplicity.
